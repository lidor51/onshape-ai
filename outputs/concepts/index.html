Assumptions and reference

P209, p.139, includes precision bends, so this proposal replaces those brackets with located flat plates and machined blocks. research/2025-coral/1690-1778.md E03, Korny's 1690 post 35, reports separating flexible acquisition from an internal orienter and restoring a rear roller after a static ceiling caused an installed dead spot. This is author-reported, not our test. Unlike 03's generic four-bar nose and single index-wheel pair, 11 explicitly follows this reference chain through seven wheels per independently driven side bank and a separately stopped, two-event cradle. 508 mm is the exact conversion of 2056's reported nominal 20-inch acquisition width, used here as mouth intent, not a measured clear opening. All other mechanism coordinates, wheel sizes, compression, bank pitch, sensor spacing and receiver geometry are our untested hypotheses.

## Scope And Evidence Rules

Keep the selected new 1+9 hybrid: deployable pickup followed by a powered plan-view V indexer. Option 1 is the passive-V concept, not evidence that the inspected 1690 indexer was passive. Option 9 requires contact/presence sensing and independent-side control. Option 14 retains its locked-carrier interpretation; blocked 1778 CAD is not a reason to reopen this design choice. Actual 1690 geometry informed the work, but its custom parts are not a manufacturing template for this design.

The design directory/generator was absent at initial inspection; the concurrently created [model.py](model.py) and [params.json](params.json) were subsequently inspected read-only. No completed assembly or new validation run was verified by this audit. Custom dimensions are mm: X width, Y into robot, Z up; blank chassis 700 x 760. Pickup axis is parallel to X at Y110/Z210; folding is -120 degrees about that axis. Roller Y/Z centers are (-140,34), (-261,161), (-136,262), (-9,287), with requested ODs 51/127/127/127. Vertical indexer shafts are at X +/-172.5/Y74, +/-110/Y182, +/-92.25/Y288. Final coral is 301.625 long x 114.3 OD, centered at Y385/Z191 above tray Z133.85; its axis is explicitly Y in the parameters. These are specified layout inputs, not manufactured measurements.

Evidence levels: **VENDOR** = cached original drawing/table; **CAD** = existing hash-bound source-geometry measurement; **DERIVED** = arithmetic from those values; **DESIGN TARGET** = proposed acceptance criterion, never a measured pass. The attachment records explicitly say assembly fit and operating clearance are unvalidated.

### Gear Mesh And Axial Stack

For **WCP-1010 + WCP-0121**, DERIVED reduction is 60/12 = **5:1**, module25.4/20 = **1.27 mm**, and nominal center distance is (12+60)/(2*20)*25.4 = **45.72 mm**. The listed family pressure angle is 14.5 degrees per the local research record; confirm it against the exact ordered pair before procurement. For shifted **WCP-1011**, the spacing rule would instead give (14+60)/40*25.4 = **46.99 mm**, despite the same 5:1 tooth ratio. Do not use outside diameters to set centers.

Use a shaft-stack section with one axial datum per motor/reducer, not a floating gear visually touching another. A **provisional layout example**, using source motor shoulder Z5.55625 and a 19.05-wide pinion seated directly there, puts pinion ends at Z5.55625/24.60625 and midplane Z15.08125. Centering a nominal9.525-wide output tooth face there puts that face at Z10.31875/19.84375. This is not a verified spline fit or released spacer dimension. Face-midplane error <=0.25 and >=9.0 axial tooth overlap are DESIGN TARGETS; relieve all other features from rotating faces by >=0.5 at worst stack.

The shaft still protrudes **12.7508 mm** beyond that illustrative pinion. A washer against the shaft tip alone would not clamp the pinion: specify a sleeve/spacer that actually bridges to the gear, then the end washer and #10-32 screw. Confirm chamfers, shoulder seating, spline usable length and thread depth using the exact pinion. Motor mount screw projection target4.5-5.5 into the6.35 blind depth; shaft-end target6.0-8.0 into9.525. These are provisional engagement windows, not vendor torque/strength approvals. Include washer thickness and screw under-head length; verify bottom clearance >=0.75 and manufacturer torque before final selection. Never bottom a screw to simulate clamp force.

For every roller/indexer shaft, draw both plate faces, both bearing flange directions, inner-race contact faces, gear/pulley/wheel widths, spacer lengths, retention shoulder/end screw or verified hex collar, and removal direction. Clamp spacers against **inner races only**, not seals or stationary outer races. One axial locating stack plus a controlled floating side avoids uncontrolled bearing preload. DESIGN TARGET axial endplay0.10-0.30 after assembly; final bearing arrangement and thermal allowance need confirmation. On vertical shafts, provide positive top and bottom retention; wheel interference is not axial retention. No generic set-screw collar on a hex corner without a compatible product/drawing and torque check.

The research describes a polycarbonate core, but this drawing labels TPU; construction/press procedure must be confirmed for the ordered variant. Actual installed OD/compression is not established.

Thus OD51 at the kicker is a rounded design envelope, **not** the exact wheel dimension; at Z34 the nominal50.8 wheel bottom is Z8.6. Model actual installed OD for traction. A row of12.7-wide disks does not provide continuous width coverage: set wheel count, axial pitch, phase and end support explicitly. Do not infer a solid roller from a sparse row of wheels.

No exact **127 mm upper roller** or **indexer wheel** SKU/drawing is bound in the supplied import packet. The 1690 reference used3-inch indexer tyres, but that is not an orderable selection or proof of compatibility. The new generator instead declares360-wide custom pickup sleeves (OD51 over hard core24; OD127 over core40) and custom OD76.2 x25.4 indexer tyres over38 mm cores, with material/hardness unselected. DERIVED opposing indexer clear gaps are268.8,143.8,108.3. The last pair would compress a114.3-OD coral by6.0 total, nominal3.0 per side at its equator. This is nominal-envelope arithmetic, not measured contact; actual diameter, hardness, wheel height and shaft position determine feasibility. The middle pair's gap exceeds114.3, so an already-longitudinal coral cannot be assumed driven there merely because wheels exist upstream/downstream. Upper/lower tyre layers do not all touch a circular coral with the same compression.

A custom tube-plus-rubber upper roller is allowed only as a clearly labeled **custom** assembly with specified tube, rubber, bonding/retention, balance/runout and service method; it is not authentic COTS.

## Drive Sizing Limits

The local motor dataset keeps WCP and CTRE, trapezoidal and FOC, separate. X44 WCP trapezoidal endpoints are7758 rpm,4.11 Nm,279 A; WCP table voltage was not established. The separate CTRE2025-09-26 test is12 V,7758 rpm,4.113 Nm,279.1 A at extrapolated stall, supply-current measurement at100% duty. No continuous torque or recommended current limit is established. FOC entitlement/control mode is not assumed. Supply current is **not** stator current at reduced duty; a breaker rating is not a motor torque limit.

For a bounded DESIGN TARGET screening example only, use CTRE trapezoidal endpoint ratio4.113/279.1 =0.0147367 Nm/A, 5:1 and assumed stage efficiency0.80. At30 A torque-producing-current proxy this estimates1.768 Nm output, at40 A2.358 Nm. Neither is a validated Talon current-limit torque result. Tangential force at a127 mm roller is about27.85/37.13 N respectively, shared among its contacts, not available independently at every wheel. At30 A the12T pinion nominal tooth tangential force is about58.0 N before losses; include belt tension, overhang and impact when sizing bearings/plates. Confirm controller telemetry definition and measure actual force/current before relying on these estimates.

Full-voltage no-load surface speed after5:1 is approximately**10.318 m/s at OD127**, **6.191 m/s at OD76.2**, and **4.127 m/s at OD50.8**. These are kinematic upper bounds, not required operating speeds. Begin low-speed commissioning and log the useful feed-speed range; a5:1 gear stage alone does not establish torque margin, controllability or safety. For any additional belt stage multiply ratios and losses. Independent V sides need separately controllable drives plus measured contact/presence state; a single common belt cannot supply differential control.

The deploy drive is not sized by this roller calculation. Required output torque includes gravity of the actual moving cassette and coral, pivot inertia times commanded acceleration, belt/cable resistance and stop loads. Record moving mass/CG/inertia and hard-stop load path; no supplier stall torque is a continuous holding rating. Do not assign the entire intake mass to a guessed pivot radius and call that measured sizing.

## Load And Electrical Budget

DESIGN TARGET mass budget: complete subsystem<=8.0 kg excluding coral/chassis, moving cassette<=4.0 kg; weighed BOM subtotal within10% of scale measurement, and100% of motors/hardware/wires/guards included. These are proposed budget gates, not measured mass or a claim that four X44 drives meet them. Parent source currently defines pickup, fold and two indexer motors; record actual quantity. Use1.0 kg as a provisional coral load case until the actual test pieces are weighed; if heavier, increase all associated load tests/calculations.

DESIGN TARGET screening load cases:150 N normal and100 N tangential distributed at pickup contacts,250 N lateral at the furthest supported mouth point, plus actual belt preload and1.0 kg piece gravity. Calculate worst deployed/stowed lever arms, fastener slip/bearing, shaft bending/torsion and plate deflection; require>=2 against material yield under the declared quasistatic operating cases. Target loaded shaft deflection<=1.0 and gear-center displacement<=0.05. These are initial cases, not field-impact ratings; a larger derived motor-limited/impact load governs. Bench proof only after safe fixturing/engineering approval, with permanent set<=0.25 and no loosened fasteners/cracks.

For illustration only,4 kg moving mass at a0.25 m horizontal CG lever requires9.81 Nm gravity torque **before** coral, acceleration or efficiency. Compare this with the provisional5:1 X44 current estimates in the COTS review; a5:1 fold stage is not automatically adequate or self-locking. Use the actual mass/CG through the fold and an independent mechanical stow stop/latch. Do not energize an undersized drive to hold a falling cassette during service.

Initial commissioning DESIGN TARGET, subject to actual motor/controller documentation: mechanically unload first, use low speed and10 A stator limit per drive; functional trials may advance to30 A stator/20 A supply per drive only after logging and torque-path checks. Hardware/controller/vendor lower limits govern. Software aggregate subsystem supply target<=60 A; voltage test points12.0 and10.0 V under a suitable current-limited source/approved battery setup. These are not continuous ratings and do not authorize holding a stalled motor. Record stator and supply separately, voltage, speed, fault flags and temperatures. Fault stop target<=0.25 s; automated jam reverse target<=0.5 s, max two attempts, then latched fault. A jam criterion needs both commanded motion and measured lack of progress, not a guessed current spike alone.

Thermal DESIGN TARGET: after ten150-second representative loaded cycles with60-second rests at25 +/-5 C ambient, controller-reported temperature<70 C, measured case<60 C, rise over ambient<=35 C, and peak case temperatures over final three cycles differ<=2 C. Stop earlier for supplier limits, rapid temperature rise, belt/tyre damage, smoke, abnormal noise or sensor faults. If no stable trend is reached, do not label duty continuous. Unattended testing is prohibited.

## Minimum Module And Service Design

Keep existing pickup, left/right V banks and tray/dock as repeatable service units; share left/right components and stock thicknesses where geometry permits. Low part count means fewer unique parts and fewer disturbed adjustments, not missing retention/guards. Count unique custom parts, all hardware, motors and maintenance operations at each release; justify each new bracket/idler by its actual load/contact/access requirement. Do not force an arbitrary part-count reduction that removes a necessary bearing or tensioner.

DESIGN TARGET module interface: one flat datum face, one round locating feature and one relieved/slot locating feature to avoid overconstraint, plus four accessible structural clamp fasteners. M5 through fasteners are a candidate, not an asserted existing pattern or adequate load rating. Locate within0.10 at the reusable datums and achieve the P10 reinstallation target. Use separate positive locators/shoulders for shear as justified by load analysis; screw clearance holes alone do not give deterministic repeatability. The source's40x40 cassette pattern and58 mm square plate are current custom geometry, not a COTS interface. Do not invent a vendor bolt pattern to match them.

For each removable rotating unit, retain two bearings, accessible correctly fitted hex/shouldered retention, premeasured spacers, captured nuts or accessible locknuts, and a tension-release provision. Arrange one removable bearing plate so a roller can leave along a documented path without extracting a shaft through the entire robot. Pair left/right V-bank mounting patterns if possible; do not couple their drives merely to save hardware. Use polarized service connectors with strain relief and labels, selected for real current/CAN/environmental needs, not an invented connector compatibility claim.

1. Disable and isolate power, mechanically support/latch the cassette, remove coral and confirm no stored spring/belt energy can move it.
2. Record current tension/datum witness marks; disconnect the labeled module connectors and release tension using the documented adjustment, not by prying a belt over a flange.
3. Remove guard and retained service fasteners, withdraw module on its defined path; keep calibrated spacer stack with that module. Do not disturb chassis datums or receiver alignment.
4. Seat replacement on clean datum face/locators; install specified grade/length fasteners to the approved torque/locking procedure, verify actual screw engagement and endplay, set tension with the selected supplier method and restore guards/connectors.
5. Hand-rotate through a full shaft turn, sweep the supported mechanism, confirm sensor states and current-limited no-load direction, then run ten documented feeds before returning to use. Reapply witness marks only after checks pass.

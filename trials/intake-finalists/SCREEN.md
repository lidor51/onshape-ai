For a horizontal cylinder the exact plan silhouette is a rectangle: axis u=(sin(alpha),cos(alpha)), normal n=(cos(alpha),-sin(alpha)); half-extents in those directions L/2 and OD/2. These tests do not cover pitch, upright/lollipop coral or compression.
